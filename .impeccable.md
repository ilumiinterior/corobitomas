## Design Context
Users: Tomáš and friends opening a joke wheel on their phones to randomly find out what Tomáš is doing. User explicitly requested an outrageous Slovak meme app, with all three supplied photos.
Brand personality: chaotic, cheeky, loud. Three words: absurd, fluorescent, tabloid.
Aesthetic: broken TV game show / photocopied meme magazine. Acid yellow canvas, ink borders, hot pink accents, expressive photo stickers, oversized condensed headings. Font defaults rejected: Space Grotesk, Syne, Inter. Catalog choices: Barlow Condensed and Nunito Sans.
Principles: wheel is the main action; all twelve outcomes have equal probability; funny Slovak copy; mobile first; sound and motion have controls; works offline after first visit.
Second pass: user explicitly requested even more absurdity and better phone presentation. Focus the chaos on spinning/reveal: photo spectators, changing brain commentary, face confetti. On phones keep the spin action fixed within thumb reach, respect safe-area insets, and bring the result into view. Support widths starting at 320px and landscape. Keep reduced-motion control.
